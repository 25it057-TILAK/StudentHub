Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common StudentHub questions.
What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a digital platform designed to connect students with academic information, events and useful student services.

How can I create an account?

Click on the Register button and fill in your basic information to create your StudentHub account.

How can I register for an event?

Open the Events page, select your preferred event and click the Register Now button.

Can I update my profile?

Yes. Login to your account and open your Profile page to update your information.

How can I send feedback?

Open the Feedback page and submit your feedback using the feedback form.

How can I contact StudentHub?

Visit the Contact page and send us your message.
